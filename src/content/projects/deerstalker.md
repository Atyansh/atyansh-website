A 3D online multiplayer brawler game developed as part of CSE 125 at UC San Diego. Players fight to knock each other off the stage using special ability "hats" that provide unique powers.

The game features networked gameplay with custom physics simulation, real-time combat, and collaborative software engineering across a team of 7 developers over a 10-week period.
