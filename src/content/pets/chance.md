# Meet Chance

Chance is my adorable 9-year-old Chorkie (Chihuahua-Yorkie mix) who has been a loyal companion for many years.

## Personality

Chance is a sweet and gentle soul. Despite his small size, he has a big personality and loves being the center of attention. He's always ready for cuddles and enjoys spending time with family.

## Fun Facts

- He's a Chorkie, a charming mix of Chihuahua and Yorkshire Terrier
- At 9 years old, he's still as playful as ever
- His favorite spot is curled up on the couch
- He has the cutest little bark

Chance is more than a pet - he's family.
