# The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

Revolutionary open-world design that gives players true freedom to explore. Every hill invites you to climb it, and the sense of discovery is unmatched.

## What Makes It Special

- Perfect physics-based gameplay
- Emergent solutions to problems
- Beautiful art direction
- Rewarding exploration

One of the best games ever made. Changed how I think about game design.
