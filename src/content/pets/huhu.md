# Meet HuHu

HuHu is my mischievous 7-year-old orange cat who keeps life interesting with his playful antics.

## Personality

HuHu is full of energy and curiosity. As an orange cat, he lives up to the stereotype of being a little troublemaker. He's always exploring, climbing, and finding new ways to entertain himself (and us!). Despite his mischievous nature, he's incredibly loving and affectionate.

## Fun Facts

- He's a classic orange tabby with a vibrant coat
- No surface is too high for him to climb
- He has a knack for knocking things off counters
- His mischievous personality keeps everyone on their toes

HuHu brings endless entertainment and joy to our home.
